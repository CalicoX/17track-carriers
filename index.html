About DHL Express

DHL Express is the international time-definite express parcel and document carrier in DHL Group. DHL Express collects shipments from shippers, operates export and import express networks, manages customs-related shipment processing where the service includes brokerage support, and delivers either through DHL Express units or local delivery partners where DHL Express uses a partner for the final mile.

Quick Answer

To track DHL Express, enter the DHL Express waybill number on DHL Tracking or use 17TRACK to monitor the same shipment in a multi-carrier dashboard. If no result appears, first confirm that the number is a DHL Express waybill rather than a merchant order ID, DHL eCommerce number, freight reference, invoice number or postal tracking number.

Pro Tip: 17TRACK is useful when a seller sends multiple tracking IDs or when a shipment may move through more than one carrier. DHL Express remains the party that transports the shipment and controls delivery changes, customs contact and claims under DHL Express terms.

How to Track DHL Express Packages

DHL Express tracking starts with a waybill number, sometimes called an air waybill or shipment number. A shipper usually receives it on the label, MyDHL+ shipment record, pickup receipt or shipping confirmation; an online buyer usually receives it in a merchant dispatch email or order page.

  • Use the official DHL Tracking page for DHL Express-only tracking, delivery proof and DHL-controlled status explanations.
  • Use 17TRACK when you want automatic carrier detection, multi-carrier visibility, normalized shipment stages or batch tracking with other carriers.
  • If tracking is empty, remove spaces or hyphens, check whether the seller provided an order number instead of a waybill, and allow time between label creation and the first DHL Express acceptance scan.
  • If the tracking number belongs to DHL eCommerce, DHL Global Forwarding, a postal operator or a marketplace logistics partner, select the correct carrier because those shipments can have different networks and support teams.

Tracking Number Formats and Examples

DHL Express primarily tracks by a DHL Express waybill number. DHL systems may also search by reference for shippers, but a reference that is searchable in an account is not the same as a carrier-issued waybill. Spaces and hyphens should be removed before lookup.

Tracking Statuses and Meanings

DHL Express statuses follow a lifecycle from shipment information received, pickup, facility processing, customs and delivery. A status must be read together with location, timestamp and whether DHL Express physically scanned the shipment.

Picked upDHL Express accepted the shipment from the shipper or pickup location.

Clearance event / Customs status updatedThe shipment is in a customs-related step and may require documents, duties, taxes or authority review.

DeliveredDHL Express recorded a delivery completion scan, often with proof of delivery or recipient/signature details where available.
Delivery attempted / Consignee not availableThe courier could not complete delivery because access, address, payment or recipient availability failed.
Returned to shipperThe shipment is being returned because delivery, customs, payment or address resolution failed under service rules.

International Shipping, Customs and Carrier Handoffs

DHL Express is an integrated express carrier, so many shipments stay within the DHL Express network from pickup to delivery. Customs authorities are separate legal decision makers, and some destinations or remote areas may involve an authorized delivery partner after DHL Express arranges the handoff.

Lost, Damaged, Claims, Insurance and Refunds

A tracking inquiry, damage report, insurance claim, postage refund and seller refund are separate processes. DHL Express claim rights usually belong to the contracting shipper or payer unless local rules or account terms allow another party to file.

FAQ

1. How do I track my DHL Express package?

Enter the DHL Express waybill number on DHL Tracking or use 17TRACK to view available shipment events in a multi-carrier dashboard.

2. Where can I find my DHL Express tracking number?

The DHL Express waybill is normally on the shipping label, shipper receipt, MyDHL+ record, merchant order page or dispatch email.

3. What does a DHL Express tracking number look like?

A common DHL Express waybill is 10 numeric digits, but references and non-DHL Express partner IDs can look different, so verify the carrier if the number does not track.

4. When will the first DHL Express tracking update appear?

The first useful physical update usually appears after DHL Express picks up or accepts the shipment, while “shipment information received” only means the shipper created data.

5. Why is my DHL Express tracking not updating?

DHL Express tracking may pause during line-haul, customs review, weekend operations, remote-area transfer or when the shipment is waiting for documents or duty payment.

6. How long does DHL Express delivery take?

DHL Express delivery time depends on origin, destination, cutoff, service, customs and local holidays; use MyDHL+ for the current lane-specific estimate or commitment.

7. What should I do after a DHL Express delivery attempt failed?

Use the DHL notification, delivery notice or DHL On Demand Delivery to arrange redelivery, pickup or delivery instructions where the shipment is eligible.

8. What if DHL Express says delivered but I did not receive the package?

Check the delivery location, reception, mailroom, household, neighbors and Proof of Delivery first, then contact DHL Express and the shipper with the waybill and dispute details.

9. How do I report a lost or damaged DHL Express shipment?

Preserve packaging, take photos, keep value proof and ask the shipper or account holder to start the DHL Express claim process when required by local terms.

10. How do I contact DHL Express customer service?

Use the DHL Customer Service page for your country and prepare the waybill, shipment date, addresses, last scan and any customs or damage evidence.

11. Is DHL Express the same as DHL eCommerce?

DHL Express and DHL eCommerce are different DHL services; DHL Express is time-definite express, while DHL eCommerce often uses economy or postal-style networks.

12. What does a DHL Express clearance event mean?

A clearance event means the shipment is in a customs-related step and may need invoice details, importer identification, duty payment or customs authority review.

13. Can a DHL Express tracking number change during international shipping?

The DHL Express waybill usually remains the main identifier, but a delivery partner or merchant system may display an additional local reference in some cases.

14. Does DHL Express provide Proof of Delivery?

DHL Express may provide Proof of Delivery where available, but signature details and visibility can depend on country, service, privacy rules and shipment status.

15. Is the DHL Express estimated delivery date guaranteed?

An estimated delivery date is not automatically a guarantee; time-definite and money-back eligibility depend on the selected service, local terms and exclusions.

16. Can businesses track DHL Express shipments through an API?

Businesses can use DHL Developer APIs for DHL Express account integrations or 17TRACK API for multi-carrier tracking, depending on whether the need is carrier-specific operations or aggregated visibility.

17. Can 17TRACK change my DHL Express delivery address or approve a claim?

No. 17TRACK is an independent tracking platform and cannot change addresses, collect duties, release customs holds, approve claims or issue merchant refunds.
